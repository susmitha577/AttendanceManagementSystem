INSTRUCTIONS TO THE STUDENT

  1. Every student has to maintain discipline in all respects.
  2. Minimum attendance of 75% is compulsory for attending the Semester Final examinations. In no case a student will be sent for End Examinations if he/she does not maintain required attendance.
  3. Every student has to pay all the fees prescribed by the Institute at the time of admission.
  4. Any damage made by the student to the Institute/Department will attract the penalty.
  5. College identity card, which will be collected every year at the end of the academic year/Semester and returned after the fee dues are cleared.
  6. Students should take care of the books issued from the Central Library.
  7. A fine will be collected for delay in returning the books issued from the Library and Hostel.
  8. Use of cell phones, smoking and ragging by students in the college premises is strictly prohibited.
  9. Disciplinary action shall be taken in case of malpractice during Internal/Final Examinations as per the Rules framed by the university/institute.
  10. Students shall submit all certificates and relevant documents before leaving the Institute.
